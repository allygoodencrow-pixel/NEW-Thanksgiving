## Data and planning corrections

- Filled measured ingredients that the outlines omitted, including eggs, leavening, flour, mayonnaise, cream, vinegar and aromatics. Amounts are normalized once from the source batch, rather than treating rough old per-person amounts as source quantities.
- Replaced roundup links with individual recipes for corn and broccoli casseroles, vegan green bean casserole, gluten-free cornbread and stuffed squash. Used the publisher's explicit vegan-butter option for squash. Almond milk and walnuts are now reflected in ingredients and dietary labels. Worcestershire in the dry-brined turkey is no longer labelled fish-free; gelatin sauce is not vegetarian.
- Real source stages now drive dry-brined turkey, stuffing, pumpkin pie and stuffed squash. Bread rise/chill, cranberry gel setting and pie cooling have planning allowances. Warm prepared rolls follow package directions. New pecan pie includes its homemade pastry ingredients and method. BA pumpkin pie uses a prepared unbaked crust; homemade dough ingredients/time are not implicitly included.
- Fixed duplicate legacy turkey timeline tasks for the newly completed dry-brined recipe. Crostini finish before guest arrival, rather than at the side-dish dinner deadline.
- Whole pies, fixed-size casseroles, rolls, stuffing and birds round to full source batches. Each batch reserves a separate oven slot; increasing portions does not multiply a single pan's cooking duration. Smaller flexible sides scale ingredients; time remains a source-size estimate. Cooking times, preparation allowances and cooling are estimates: use the complete linked method and check actual doneness. Brining and other advance work stay explicit in make-ahead notes.
- Costs for researched source ingredients are deliberately unpriced; the existing Budget incomplete warning remains until real unit prices are entered. Optional garnishes, pan grease, extra flour, ice and to-taste seasoning are clearly noted rather than given fabricated measured quantities.
- Highly Rated uses numeric scores of at least 4.5/5. Rating and dietary filters now apply to the source collection as well as serving/personal recipes. Source cards and readers display the verified score and sample size.
- Methods are concise original planning summaries, with direct links to the complete publisher instructions. The 15 supplied source recipes were not rewritten or assigned invented ratings.
